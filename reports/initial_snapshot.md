# Initial price snapshot

This aggregate summary uses the user-supplied Mamaearth HTML pages parsed offline. It is a one-time snapshot, not a live fetch and not a sale-history analysis. The product offers list `2026-10-27` as `validFrom`; the exact browser save times were not independently verified.

## Result

Ten in-stock product pages contained both a product-specific current price and a product-specific strikethrough MRP. The offline collector parsed all 10 pages successfully with no rejected or blocked rows. Their advertised discounts, calculated as `(MRP - current price) / MRP`, range from **10.0% to 20.0%**. The mean is **14.9%** and the median is **14.0%** (rounded to one decimal place).

## Scope and exclusions

The supplied folder contained 16 product pages after the Mineral Based Sunscreen page was added. One Rice Night Cream page reported out of stock. Five other pages did not provide both price fields for their own product offer in the saved page data, so they were excluded. The saved Charcoal Face Wash is a different product from the held Tea Tree/Charcoal candidate and is used as the replacement in the 10-product catalog.

## Limits

This describes only prices shown in these saved page snapshots. It cannot establish historical prices, sale timing, or whether an advertised discount reflects a genuine sale. No product-level price rows are published in this report. Live collection has not been run; the private data repository and owner-authorized go-live gates remain necessary for ongoing tracking.
